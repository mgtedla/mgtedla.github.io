Mebrahtu G. Tedla

Immunologist | Translational & Systems Immunology | Computational Immunology | Precision Medicine

I am an immunology researcher interested in understanding immune dysregulation, loss of immune tolerance, and mechanisms underlying autoimmune disease progression.

My research integrates experimental immunology, animal models, flow cytometry, immunometabolism, transcriptomics, and computational approaches to investigate disease mechanisms and identify translational biomarkers.

Research Interests

Autoimmunity & Type 1 Diabetes

Investigating mechanisms that contribute to immune tolerance loss and progression of autoimmune diabetes.

B-cell Biology

Studying B-cell development, activation, tolerance checkpoints, and their contribution to autoimmune disease.

Immunometabolism

Exploring how metabolic reprogramming influences immune-cell phenotype, activation, and function.

Systems & Computational Immunology

Applying transcriptomics, single-cell analysis, bioinformatics, and integrative computational approaches to immunological questions.

Selected Research Projects

Type I Interferon and B-cell Immunometabolism

Investigating how Type I interferon signaling alters B-cell metabolism, immune activation, and tolerance during the development of Type 1 diabetes.

B-cell Transcriptional Signatures

Using RNA sequencing and computational analysis to identify B-cell transcriptional programs and molecular pathways associated with autoimmune disease progression.

Immune Biomarker Discovery

Exploring immune, transcriptomic, and metabolic signatures that may help identify early disease changes and support precision medicine approaches.
