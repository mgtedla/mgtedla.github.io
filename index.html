About Me

I am an immunology researcher interested in understanding mechanisms of immune dysregulation, autoimmune disease progression, and immune tolerance. My work integrates experimental immunology, animal models, flow cytometry, transcriptomics, and computational approaches.

Research Interests

My research interests include Type 1 diabetes, B-cell biology, immune tolerance, immunometabolism, translational immunology, systems immunology, and precision medicine.
